BLUE HOURA 15-minute browser game · 15분짜리 브라우저 게임

What it is

BLUE HOUR is a 15-minute narrative game set in December 2029, in a neighborhood under white towers, where the Agents make every decision and the law still requires one human signature on each. You are that signature for three days; after the last night, the you of 2029 writes a letter to the you of 2026.

Human rights were not abolished.
Support has simply ended.인권은 폐지되지 않았다. 지원 종료되었을 뿐이다.

The master copy. Use both lines together, unchanged, in either language.

  • About 15 minutes. Tap to continue. Built for phones first.
  • Free. Browser only. No login, no download, no account.
  • Progress is saved in the player's browser. The page sends the name, the 2026 job, the progress and the signature counts to the maker. Diary answers and the letter itself are never sent.
  • Korean original, with English, Chinese, Japanese and Spanish builds. The city changes per language; the neighborhood has no name in any of them.
  • On screen, the job title is Attestor. Your agent is ON. The company's agents are the Agents.
  • Play: bluehour2029.github.io/en.html

Streaming guide, spoiler-free

Show on stream

  • The job pick and the HRV card. Choose your real 2026 job. The card gives it a number out of 100 and one line of reasoning. Read the number aloud. Plumber / Electrician scores 57. Developer scores 31.
  • The first batch case. № 01 on Day 1: 384 standard cases, one stamp. Open the basis line; it says reading them would take 9 hours 40 minutes. Then decide on camera.
  • The bungeoppang stall. Evening, 4F Alley. Next to the bungeoppang robot, a human bakes. The human line is longer. Buy one.
  • The vote. Day 3, the polling station opens for one day. Vote, leave without voting, or walk past. All three are recorded.

Keep off stream

The letter text. The letter is the ending and it is written to the player. Show the card if you like; do not read the text on camera. Ask viewers to go get their own. After the third night come the diary and a short recap of December 24. The letter follows the recap. Cut there.

보여줘도 되는 것

  • 직업 선택과 산정 카드. 진짜 2026년 직업을 고르세요. 카드가 100점 만점의 숫자와 산정 사유 한 줄을 줍니다. 숫자를 소리 내어 읽으세요. 배관공·전기기사 57, 개발자 31.
  • 첫 일괄 건. 1일차 № 01. 표준 건 384건에 도장 하나. 근거를 열면 열람 시 9시간 40분이 걸린다고 적혀 있습니다. 카메라 앞에서 정하세요.
  • 붕어빵 노점. 저녁, 4층 골목. 붕어빵 로봇 옆에 사람이 굽는 노점이 있습니다. 사람 쪽 줄이 더 깁니다. 하나 사세요.
  • 투표. 3일차, 투표소가 하루만 열립니다. 기표, 기표 없이 나오기, 지나치기. 셋 다 기록됩니다.

보여주지 않는 것

편지 본문. 편지는 이 게임의 끝이고, 플레이어에게 쓰인 글입니다. 카드는 보여도 좋습니다. 본문은 읽지 마세요. 시청자에게 직접 받으라고 하세요. 셋째 밤 뒤에 일기와 12월 24일의 짧은 결산이 나오고, 그다음이 편지입니다. 거기서 끊으세요.
